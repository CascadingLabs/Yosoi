Record conceptual steps separately from executable examples. Do not format those steps as code. Rust and Python panels must use the same fixture and intended result where both interfaces support the task.

## Reader review

- Does the opening say what the reader will understand or accomplish?
- Does each major idea have a small real example or clear numbered steps?
- Does the explanation walk through concrete calls and returned values?
- Are expected output, missing data and unsuccessful outcomes explained?
- Is shared behavior explained once?
- Are syntax differences and capability gaps visible?
- Are ownership, resource bounds and cleanup explained where relevant?
- Do recipe checks prove the business result?
- Are install/run instructions Cargo or uv?
- Is the document plain Markdown without em dashes?
