# Locators and findings

[Making sense of HTML](documents-and-evidence.md) develops locators through the lamp's name, price, and product link. It explains how a Plan separates the selection choices from evaluation and shows findings from changed input.

[Following the data](following-the-data.md) extends the same vocabulary to JSON Pointer, JSONPath, namespaced XML, and plain-text matches. [Adding a product to the cart](adding-to-the-cart.md) introduces roles and accessible names through the shop's controls.

A finding keeps its value connected to the named output, Document, and evidence location. The worked lessons show those relationships where the task uses them.
