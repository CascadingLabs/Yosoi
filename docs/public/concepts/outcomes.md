# Outcomes

[Bounding the work](policy-and-budgets.md) demonstrates an exhausted limit, a failed prerequisite that prevents a click, and cancellation before acquisition. It also explains why an interrupted browser action can leave its effect unknown.

[Building a product record](contracts-and-validation.md) retains an accepted product alongside a rejected candidate. [Adding a product to the cart](adding-to-the-cart.md) checks the intended cart values before accepting the recipe as complete.

Use the outcome appropriate to the task. Returning from an operation, obtaining complete evidence, accepting every candidate, and reaching the intended browser state establish different facts.
